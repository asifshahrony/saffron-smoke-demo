Our Culinary Heritage

Crafted by Fire, Perfumed with Hand-Harvested Spice

Founded in 2019 by Executive Chef Julian Vance, Saffron & Smoke celebrates the primal poetry of live-fire cooking balanced with refined botanical aromatics. Every sourdough crust, prime cut, and reduction begins in our custom brick hearth.

Grade-A Saffron

Single-origin crimson threads steeped daily for sauces, risottos, and signature spritzes.

Seasoned White Oak

Slow-cured hardwood embers impart a clean, subtle smokiness to meats and blistered dough.
